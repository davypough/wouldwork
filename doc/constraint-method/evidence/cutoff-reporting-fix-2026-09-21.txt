Approved parallel cutoff reporting and unknown-coverage correction
================================================================
Worker DFS now mirrors serial DF-BNB1 at a depth-cutoff node: test whether it
has successors, retain a worker-local positive flag, and skip later probes
for that worker once the flag is true. The successors are neither enqueued
nor checked for goals beyond the allowed depth. Aggregate flags after workers
finish, preserving any already recorded positive global flag.

This adds no shared write or lock on the cutoff path. Probe overhead is not
benchmarked: a worker with no positive witness will examine each terminal
cutoff node, while a worker finding a witness stops probing. This mirrors the
serial semantics without pretending a cutoff hit alone proves truncation.

Ledger provenance now accepts T, NIL, or :UNKNOWN. Omitted ingestion data
defaults to :UNKNOWN; the reporter no longer treats any non-NIL value as T.
Invalid supplied values signal. Reliable NIL is described only as no observed
direct cutoff truncation, because pruning can also limit explored states.
The raw historical COMPLETE/NIL result remains recorded separately from its
corrected UNKNOWN coverage interpretation. LK5 is OPEN with bound BD2.
