> SD  SERVICES AND SETUP DEPENDENCIES  [grade 2]
> --------------------------------------------------------------
>   SCOPE: a monotone premise closure over passage services (every gate; each drive a traversal clause names) and receiver literals.  Providers: S1 CONTROL options, MC jam sites (OVERRIDE), gears with no fan (EQUIPMENT), RC chains and fixed corridors to a receiver.  No ordering, simultaneity, bodies, reach, occupancy or view is checked.
>   CLASSES: DIRECT no premise; SUPPORTED premises available without this service; NEEDS <service> FIRST: installing this provider needs the service it will provide (a setup dependency); UNSUPPORTED IN SCOPE.  A service with only NEEDS-FIRST options is a SETUP QUESTION, not an impossibility.  "Through standing providers" repeats the test with premises supplied only by CONTROL, chains, corridors and equipment, never another jam, and is printed when that exposes a setup dependency a further jammer would hide.
