2.2 Diagnosis (A)
a) STOPPED @ 9 is an expectation error, not a code fault.  E6 guessed s(9) at 1.5 to 4
   million from T23's fresh-staging ratios (2.5 to 6.4); from the 80-action checkpoint the
   early ratios are about 10 and s(9) exceeds 20 million.  The guard itself worked in a
   real 16-thread search: limit 20,000,000, stopped at 20,004,564 (overshoot 0.02%).
b) E4 fails mainly on b.  Bytes per state fall with depth, 643 at cutoff 4 to 167 at
   cutoff 9; the marginal cost levels at 150 to 165.  The early runs carry fixed costs
   (tables sized before they fill) spread over few states.  Spec 14.3 takes the maximum
   over runs of at least 10,000 states, which picks cutoff 4's 643: about 3.8 times the
   deep runs' cost.  This is a defect of the rule, not of the code.
c) The state projection to 10 is two steps from cutoff 8, because the STOPPED run is not a
   growth run: 6,538,357 x 3.85^2 = 96.9 million.  With b 176 (the deeper of the two
   deepest runs) M(10) is still about 17.1 GB, LIKELY TO EXHAUST.  The record cannot
   settle this: the T10 cutoff-10 run's state count and peak heap were not recorded
   (9,995,969 cutoff hits is a lower bound on states at depth 10 only).
d) A's error in A3's refinement: "completed" shows only that the cutoff-10 run's peak
   stayed under the ceiling; LIKELY TO EXHAUST starts at 80%, so the record does not
   exclude it.  E4 as refined was not settled by the record.
