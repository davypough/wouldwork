# T35 — Fixed beams, jammers and stairs

Approved and completed 2026-09-27. Specification 8.9 written before code.

## Result and scope

MC now covers beam-direct, jammer and stairs with source-grounded contracts
and instance rows. Fixed-beam records feed both MC and RC, independently of
connector availability. H3 adds a CANDIDATE for each fixed receiver corridor.
Claustro has zero UNCOVERED mechanics; both RC/H3 name gate1 and location2
for transmitter1 -> receiver1. Endpoint chromas are blue/blue; the initial
physical corridor is blocked. Records retain BEAM-VIA presence/obstacles and
LOS-BARRIER-CROSSINGS>, distinguishing absent from empty data.

Gate candidates are qualified by finite spans and interpolated beam height;
a location blocks only when an occupant spans the beam. Corridor clearance
is not receiver activation: chroma, BEAM-CUT, upstream repeater lighting and
other beam routes matter. No mixed-chain enumeration, recording-view result,
propagation, stable arrangement or reachability is claimed.

Jammer rows list ground at all locations, fixed plate sites and staged box
tops, evaluated with all gates open and each gate closed separately. They
retain each directional JAM-DISALLOWED> triple. Reach, legal support use,
occupancy, availability and the actual view still require separate checks.
Moved/stacked supports, trays and fans are not surveyed. Stairs rows preserve
the authored direction and alternative clause family; their contract requires
passability and a safe destination, not an elevation limit.
