WHY THE CANDIDATE MUST BE STAGED
The source-derived action skeleton contains four independent commitments:

  B1  first-cycle transfer of tray1 to plate1 and clean cancellation;
  B2  box1 transfer to plate2 and the second recorder fork;
  B3  ghost plate3 source keeper plus live connector1 source pairing;
  B4  live tray1/box1 transfer to plates4/5 and the empty-handed gate6 crossing.

The skeleton contains 29 nominal actions when every transparent multi-segment
route is one MOVE action: B1 9, B2 6, B3 5, B4 9. This is not a measured
length or a claim that each route is the chosen engine route. It shows why a
single fresh-start experiment cannot fit the standing cutoff ceiling of 12.
Each milestone, however, has a candidate skeleton no longer than 9 actions.

Start from the initial state. The candidate action shape is:

  START-RECORDER;
  agent1* moves within R1 and steps onto plate1;
  agent1 crosses physical gate1, picks up tray1 from plate2,
  returns through gate1, and puts tray1 on plate1;
  agent1 returns empty-handed to recorder1 and CANCEL-PLAYBACK.

Candidate postcondition:

  (and (recorder-cycle-ended)
       (on tray1 plate1)
       (has-location agent1 location1))

The nominal count is 9. The suggested future cap is 12, the existing maximum.
Success establishes only this closed baseline boundary. An exhaustion would be

another gate1 baseline.
