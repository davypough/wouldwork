SOURCE RESTORATION CANDIDATE
The two-cycle baseline leaves tray1 on plate1 and box1 on plate2 before its
second recorder start. Its new ghost tray1* and box1* therefore keep recording
gates1 and3 open. The new ghost agent can:

1. leave R1 through recording gate1;
2. enter R2 through recording gate3; and
3. make the explicit local STEP onto plate3 at location10.

Physical plate pressure is layer-blind. Thus agent1* on plate3 opens physical
gate4 as well as recording gate4. This substitutes for the live agent's
plate3 role in the saved source phase. The live agent remains free to reach
connector1 at location9 and establish the already measured pairing shape:

  transmitter1 -> connector1 at location9 -> repeater1.

The earlier beam analysis established that the low transmitter1-to-location9
segment crosses gate4 and needs physical gate4 open. With agent1* on plate3,
that condition is supplied without moving tray1 off plate1 or box1 off plate2.
The resulting candidate endpoint is:

  - tray1 on plate1, box1 on plate2, and agent1* on plate3;
  - connector1 at location9, paired to transmitter1 and repeater1;
  - repeater1 red in the physical view;
  - agent1 free and empty-handed after the connection;
  - physical and recording gates1, 3, and 4 open.

This is a source-derived candidate endpoint, not a measured action sequence.
The prior source phase demonstrates the connector pairing and red result with
the live agent on plate3; it does not validate this ghost-keeper substitution.

CORRECTION: LIVE CARGO IS FREE AFTER THE SECOND FORK
The initial resource reading incorrectly treated tray1 and box1 as permanently
committed once their ghost copies witness recording gates1 and3. That would be
true before the second START-RECORDER, but not during its open cycle. The fork
creates tray1* and box1* on the two plates. Live and ghost occupants may share
a support, and physical plate pressure is layer-blind. The live actor may move
the live cargo on its own recording side while the ghost copies remain on
plates1/2. Those ghosts continue to hold both recording and physical gates1/3
open.

box1 from plate2 to plate5. The required routes use the still-open physical
gates1 and3 and physical gate5, which remains open while switch2 is off. The
two placements leave agent1 empty-handed. The resulting source-derived
candidate endpoint is:

  tray1*       plate1: recording and physical gate1 witness

  agent1*      plate3: physical gate4 witness for the low source beam
  connector1   location9, paired to transmitter1 and repeater1
  tray1        plate4: physical gate6 witness

plate, and moving the live member leaves the ghost witness in place. Gate6
