    route choice to future access. A milestone must preserve enough resources

  - Candidate lower bound: shortest gate-crossing distance to the goal with
    all gates treated as open, under unchanged unit crossing costs. Every real
    route has at least that many crossings; ignoring box operations weakens
    this bound but does not overestimate. Stronger box-delivery bounds require
    proof against reuse and alternative routes; do not double-count actions.
  - Candidate duplicate sharing: retain the cheapest arrival at a complete
    arrangement. This could reduce repeated work compared with ancestor-only
    checking, at additional memory cost; engine cost handling needs an audit
    before choosing it for the scaled design.
