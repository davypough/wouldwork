PRELIMINARY PROJECTION (deduction, not measured)
  At 3-8 distinct new states per level beyond depth 5, depth 15 means roughly 10^9 to 10^13
  states.  Heap is about 25 GB, so a graph search holds on the order of tens of millions of
  states.  A single depth-15 forward search is very likely impractical unless growth beyond
  depth 5 falls far below the measured shallow rate.

PROPOSED NEXT PROBE (awaiting approval)

FINDINGS
  Depth-15 forward search: seven more levels at x5 to x8 per level in time gives about one
    month to several years; stored states grow to roughly 10^9 to 10^10, far above what a 25 GB
    heap holds (bytes per state not measured here).  Impractical by orders of magnitude.
  Proving 15 minimal (exhausting depth 14) is the same scale.
  Topology lower bound: at most removes the last two or three levels for states with no
    pairing path to receiver1; realistically about one level.  Not enough.
  First-move splitting (*branch*): min-length per first move, then the best of them, keeps
    the shortest-plan guarantee, but the total work is unchanged.  Useful only to spread runs.

DIRECTION
  Evaluate the direct alternatives, meet-in-the-middle from generated goal states first.

RELAXATION (source reading, 2026-10-12)
  Derived facts (colors, receivers, gate, crossings) are functions of the base facts, so removing
    them from search merges no states; loosened preconditions (e.g. gate1 open replaced by a
    pairing-chain test) can only add states.  Saving is per-state propagation cost only (share
    unmeasured; at most a constant factor), against a gap of thousands.
  Relaxed rules admit every real plan, so the relaxed shortest length is a lower bound: a relaxed
    search exhausting depth 14 would prove 15 minimal -- but it is at least as large as the real one.
  The Talos techs propagate inside their own actions, so a relaxed variant needs problem-local
    replacements for all connector and move actions (spec-advisor work).
  Not recommended for this problem.
  Observation (corrected below): probs/problem-corner-relaxed.lisp's header reports a solution
    found in about 400 s at cutoff 14 -- a first find, not an exhausted search, on different
    geometry.  Its model has no put-without-connect, no retaining pickup and no 1-terminus connect
    (connect places the held connector), and its goal names the known answer's layout.

ENUMERATOR DECLARATIONS (analysis, 2026-10-12; nothing written or run)
  Base facts in this spec: has-location (agent1, connector1-3), holding (agent1 connector),
    paired (connector terminus).  Fixed apparatus has coordinates, not has-location.
  Declarations needed:
    has-location: connectors may be unassigned (held), at most one.
    holding: its own base relation, at most one connector, only one with no location.  Required
      because :on-assign does not set facts for an unassigned key, and pickup/put/connect
      preconditions read holding, so predecessors through those actions need it.
    paired: up to 3 per connector (matches *max-connector-pairings*); a held connector may keep
      pairings (retaining pickup), unlike the old model's declaration.
  Goal filter: none needed; the spec's own goal (agent at location4, receiver2 and receiver3
    active) is neutral.  The old corner filter named the known layout and is not reused.
  Size estimate: agent fixed at location4; per connector 4 locations or held, 64 pairing subsets
    (0-3 of 7 termini); about 3 x 10^7 combinations, about 5 x 10^6 after connector symmetry,
    each propagated once: roughly 15 minutes serial at ~170 us each.  Goal-state count unknown.
  Main risk: growth of the backward layers (forward branching here is 10-18 per level).
  Threads: enumerator steps not checked with threads; run them serially until checked.

ACTION-SET REDUCTION (analysis, 2026-10-12; nothing changed)
  Requirement: dropping an action keeps min-length meaningful only if every shortest plan using
    it has a replacement plan of the same length without it.  Without that argument a reduced
    model still yields valid plans (an upper bound), but not minimality.
  Single-terminus connect: cannot be dropped.  The known 15-action plan uses it (connector2 to
    transmitter1 at location4, the cutter beam).
  Retaining pickup: probably replaceable -- plain pickup, then connect at the destination to the
    same termini, costs the same two actions -- when the connector has at most 3 links in all and
    each terminus is connectable from there.  Exceptions (more than 3 links) exist; not proved.
    About 4% of successors at depth 3.
  Put without pairing: no general replacement.  Connecting instead creates beams that can cut
    others or change colors, and an unpaired placed connector can later be linked by another
    connector's connect.  Any proof would be geometry-specific.  At most about 15% of successors
    at depth 3 (moves and puts together).
  Conclusion: removing the replaceable actions saves perhaps 5-20%, not orders of magnitude.  The
    branching is in connect choices (70%), which the spec advisor found no sound way to narrow.
  Not recommended.  Direction: return to the enumerator meet-in-the-middle, which changes no rules.

PREDECESSOR FIX, PHASE 1 (2026-10-12, engine edit approved; src/ww-enumerator-run.lisp)
  Single-state test (known final state as the only frontier, move + pickup-connector):
    before: 968 candidates, 0 validated (pickup never applicable; move applied 6 times, all to
    other locations).  No hidden errors.
  Move: no move predecessor exists for the known final state -- from location3 the agent cannot
    walk to location4 after connector2's pickup.  Correct, not a bug.
  Cause: the per-object CSP re-guessed one object at a time; undoing a pickup changes agent1's
    holding and connector2's location/pairings together.  Holding's unkeyed enumeration step was

    63 pickup predecessors with connector2 at location4 and agent1 empty-handed, including the
    known one (paired connector2 transmitter1).  Pickup branching is inherent: it erases pairings,
    so any pairing set for the picked-up connector is a valid predecessor.
  Limitation: pairings from other connectors to the picked-up connector are not restored.
