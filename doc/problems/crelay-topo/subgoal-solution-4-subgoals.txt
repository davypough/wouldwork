Crelay-Topo Subgoal Solutions
=============================

This is the cumulative record of validated subgoal solutions.  Append each new
subgoal after it has been found and validated.

The state lists describe subgoal boundaries: before setup and after all
integrated actions, including the ghost's return and STOP-RECORDER in
Subgoals 1 and 2. They list relevant true propositions, not every static fact.
PAUSE and RESUME mark the interleaving of recording and playback; they are
explanatory markers, not additional WouldWork actions.

Validation evidence: subgoal3-complete-validation.txt records successful
94-action replay and independent recorder validation; the Subgoal 3
checkpoint state is captured in subgoal3-handoff-validation.txt. No minimum-length
claim is made.
