THREE THINGS THE FIRST REAL USE CAUGHT.  All three were the assistant's, none was forced
through, and the third is an engine fact the method now carries:

     predecessor, and lk2 on pr11.  The checker signalled "grade-2 LK2 rests on search
     result PR11; no inductive proof may depend on a cutoff."  Right refusal: a chained
     milestone's SEARCH starts from its predecessor's end state, but its CLAIM is a graph
     fact independent of how the predecessor was realized.  Chaining is operational and
     belongs on :SEARCH-START, not in :DEPENDS-ON.

  2  THE RECOMMENDER PRINTED A STAGING PREAMBLE FOR EVERY CHAINED MILESTONE, so the second
     crossing's recommendation told the reader to stage -- discarding the chain it
     continues.  Links now carry :SEARCH-PREAMBLE; a :CONTINUE link emits the SOLVE-SUBGOAL
     line alone.

  3  THE ASSISTANT GAVE A WRONG RETRIEVAL COMMAND and D's REPL rejected it:
     (solution.path (first *solution-paths*)) signalled a TYPE-ERROR on NIL.  Reading
     src/ww-goal-chaining.lisp shows why, and it is by design.  COMMIT-GENERIC-GOAL-CHAIN
     sets *SOLUTION-PATHS* to NIL for a MID-CHAIN commit: there is no solution until the
     chain finishes.  A phase's own path lives in the session:
       (solution.path (goal-chain-phase-solution <phase>))
     with the chain's origin at (goal-chain-session-origin-state *goal-chain-session*).
     Only the FINAL commit publishes a cumulative path -- MAKE-GOAL-CHAIN-CUMULATIVE-
     SOLUTION over all phases -- and it also restores *START-STATE* to the origin.
     CONSEQUENCE FOR T10, and it is a simplification: the engine composes the chain
     itself, so the composition check at the end is ONE validate call, not a hand-built
     concatenation.  Recommendations now print the right retrieval for all three cases,
     with an acceptance case per case.  Suite: 49 cases, 322 assertions.
