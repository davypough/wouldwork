CONCLUSION
The nominal six-action B2 skeleton omitted a concrete elevation prerequisite
on its first box-retrieval leg. An open gate2 does not make a ground-level
jump into location6 legal. The simple blower fallback also omits a support
requirement between switching off and crossing. These source-derived local
counterexamples reject that construction as written; they do not prove B2
unreachable, diagnose every branch of the measured search, or establish an
alternative sequence. Withdraw the six-action B2 estimate and the combined
29-action skeleton as execution guidance. B3/B4 remain conditional proposals
whose B2 starting arrangement has not been realized.

1. FIRST UNSUPPLIED PRECONDITION: GAINING ELEVATION
Authored coordinates: locations4/5 are at level0; location6 and location20
are at level3/2. Box1 remains at location6 in the saved B1 endpoint.
The authored jumping edges into location6 come from locations4/5/20.
JUMP-ELEVATION-REACHABLE requires target minus launch elevation <= the
vertical reach limit, which is 1. JUMP-SEGMENT-FOR-CLAUSE checks this even
when all barrier gates are open. Thus the two ordinary ground launches have
3/2 > 1 and fail. Walking requires equal endpoint levels and cannot replace
that jump. The level location20 launch would satisfy the landing-height test,
but reaching and retaining that elevated launch position was not supplied.

S3 explicitly warns that region membership does not establish concrete
mobility: its quotient does not evaluate the modes' height predicates.
R8 includes location20 with the lower approach locations. Treating that
region as freely traversable was the proposal's error. Reading gate2's S4
controller row alone did not discharge the movement obligation.

2. REMOTE PICKUP IS NOT A SUBSTITUTE FROM THESE APPROACH LOCATIONS
PICKUP-BOX calls PICKUP-CLEAR, requiring manipulation reach and vertical
reach. The horizontal reach limit is 5/2. Location6 is 29/10 horizontally
from locations4 and20; from location5 the squared separation is 941/100,
also greater than (5/2)^2 = 625/100. Therefore no derived reach edge from
those three approach locations lets the actor simply lift box1 across the
gap. Raising the actor at the same named location does not change that
location-based horizontal test. This is a local statement about those
locations, not an exhaustive transport impossibility claim.

3. BLOWER FALLBACK HAS A PROPAGATED STATE BOUNDARY
Blower1 is at location4 and aims at location20. STEP onto it is an explicit
configuration-changing MOVE; when active, propagation launches the actor to
location20. Walking there and mounting it cannot be silently counted as one
transparent route. Ordinary grounded WALK/JUMP segments may compose in one
MOVE if every segment is legal in that state; the issue is not that every
segment necessarily costs an action.

Switch1 controls blower1 normally and gate2 inversely. In the same actor's
environmental view, an active blower and open gate2 are incompatible under
this wiring. The gate's base is 3/2 and its default height is 4: a closed
gate2 has top11/2. From location20 at3/2 it requires clearance4 > limit1,
so merely vaulting the closed gate from that hover position fails too.

Switch1 can be manipulated from location20: the profile lists the reach
candidate and its default fixture base1 differs from actor base3/2 by1/2.
But TOGGLE-SWITCH propagates immediately. When its actor's blower view stops,
DROP-OCCUPANTS! returns an unsupported occupant at location20 to location4.
There is no retained hover state in which to take the next gate2 jump.
An independent support or other specifically established transition is
needed to bridge this boundary; none is supplied by the audited skeleton.

Ghosts do not automatically evade this conjunction: both gate openness and
blower activity select the recording view for ghost objects. Live-only
switch changes cannot be borrowed as recording gate state. Ghost switches
also affect the physical switch, so any proposed split-view mechanism needs
its own explicit sequence and view-by-view checks.

4. RECORDER BOUNDARY CHECK
B1 is genuinely closed and ghost-free, with cycle count1 and agent1 at
recorder1/location1. START-RECORDER permits a new cycle at that boundary
with an empty-handed live actor; there is no configured cycle maximum.
It copies current live locations and ON assignments. Thus IF live tray1
and box1 are first established on plates1/2, the proposed fork mechanism
has source support. That conditional does not supply the missing box route.

The B2 goal did not require cycle count2, so the measured exhaustion should
not be described as testing only paths with exactly two total cycles.
Temporary extra cycles were not excluded by that goal. Any candidate that
uses them must pay for START, legal closure, exact snapshot placement and
mandatory ended-cycle validation. Cancellation at the recorder removes ghost
references; it does not preserve a ghost witness into the next fork.

The log reports zero nominal goal candidates; it does not report a completed
B2 candidate rejected by final validation. Boundary-prefix checks rejected
1388 states, but those aggregate counts do not identify the cause of failure.
The no-progress check is attached to completed cycle endings, not a blanket
