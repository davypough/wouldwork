METHOD AND PROOF RULES
----------------------
This is the constraint level above forward and backward action search:
  static profile -> bodies x roles x recorder segments -> abstract skeleton
  -> short concrete realizations -> validation of their composition.
It does not replace either direction.  A constraint proves impossibility;
a concrete action sequence proves possibility.  Neither substitutes for
the other.  Do not reason from knowledge that a solution exists.

Grades:
  1 DEFINITIONAL: follows from predicate definitions and static facts.
  2 INDUCTIVE: initial case and preservation by every applicable action.
  3 COST BOUND: exhaustion at a cutoff, relative to its initial state;
    never an invariant or an impossibility proof.
  4 TRACE/ORDERING: a statement about plans, with its own proof obligation.
No grade-2 proof may depend on a search cutoff.  Graph candidates and
conditional bounds must remain labeled with their assumptions.

C1  Write extractors from the instance and tech semantics only, without
    importing the hand analysis's answers.
C2  Score the FIRST completed run.  A later correction earns another score;
    never replace the original result.  Failed staging is not a run.
C3  No problem object names in diagnostic code.  Named substrate interfaces
    are allowed when declared and justified; S1, S3, S4 and RO use them.
    RO additionally takes every problem-specific term from a caller-supplied
    scenario, which is data and not code.  The G14 fix names nothing: it
