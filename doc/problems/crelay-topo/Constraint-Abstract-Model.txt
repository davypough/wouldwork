WHAT THE MODEL IS
-----------------
  objects     the support occupants
  roles       the slots that must be held
  segments    time cut by recorder cycles
  operators   the abstract transitions between assignments
  constraints S1-S7, plus what falls out of their join

A candidate abstract plan is an assignment of bodies to roles per
segment.  This file has the objects, the role INVENTORY, the segment
supply function, and one constraint joining them.  It does not have
the mandatory role set, and it does not have the operators.

2.1  ZERO-COST DEVICES (4).  A switch holds its own state; no body is
     consumed to keep the device in its state.

       gate2     == (not switch1)
       gate5     == (not switch2)
       gate7     == switch2
       blower1   == switch1

     S1 supplies two exclusion pairs; these are not their only
     operational constraints:

       {gate5, gate7}    on ((switch2))
       {blower1, gate2}  on ((switch1))

     Both are grade 1.  The second was carried only narratively before
     S1 computed it (7.1).

2.2  BODY-COST DEVICES (5 gates, 8 plates, disjoint).

       gate1  <- plate1                     cost 1
       gate3  <- plate2                     cost 1
       gate4  <- plate3                     cost 1
       gate6  <- plate4 AND plate5          cost 2
       gate9  <- plate6 AND plate7 AND plate8   cost 3
                                            ---------
                                            total 8

     THE COST IS CONTINUOUS, NOT ONE-SHOT.  plate.lisp's
     UPDATE-PLATE-STATUS! asserts (depressed ?p) under
     (support-occupied ?p) and denies it otherwise, on every
     propagation. Removing the last eligible witness from any required
     plate closes its listed gate in the same transition. Grade 1:
     it is the axiom defining the derived
     relation, not an induction.

     DISTINCT PLATES NEED DISTINCT WITNESSES; A PLATE MAY HOLD BOTH LAYERS.
     SUPPORT-OCCUPIED is an existential over occupants with
     (on ?occupant ?support), and ON is keyed by the occupant, so the
     map from an occupied support to a witness occupant is injective
     (S2, W7).  Grade 2.

     LAYER-BLIND.  SUPPORT-OCCUPIED applies no layer test, and S2
     attributes DEPRESSED eight witnesses on that ground (7.13).  A
     ghost depresses a plate exactly as a live body does.  This is the
     correction G6 was opened for and it is load-bearing here: the
     budget in section 5 is eight and not four.

2.3  BEAM DEVICE (1 gate).

       gate8  <- receiver1                  cost NOT YET A NUMBER

     gate8 is the only device S1 places at depth 2, and S1 says plainly
     that the identity of the supplying device is not decidable from
     the control algebra.  receiver1 is energized by ACTIVE, which
     UPDATE-RECEIVER-STATUS! asserts through the beam queries; those
     read ON and HAS-LOCATION, both layer-blind through BASE (7.15).

     If a connector relay is needed and cannot simultaneously serve a
     required plate role, it draws from the same occupant pool. Neither
     relay necessity nor role incompatibility follows from the count.
     Beam geometry and any riser requirement remain for S5/S6.
     RECORDED AS A CONDITIONAL COST, not as a number, because naming
     one now would be an invention.  See section 7.


3  SEGMENTS                                            [SUPPLY
                                                        FUNCTION
                                                        COMPLETE;
                                                        NO EXTRACTOR
                                                        OWNS IT -- G8]
------------------------------------------------------------------
Body supply is a step function of the segment, not a constant.
-recorder-cycle-boundary.lisp's REMOVE-RECORDER-GHOST-STATE! removes
every dynamic proposition referring to a mapped ghost at a cycle
boundary, and RECORDER-CLOSED-GHOST-FREE is asserted over the result.
At start, S2's reading of *START-STATE* shows HAS-LOCATION assigning
four of eight keys; the four unassigned are the ghosts, which do not
exist until a fork makes them (W13).

    outside an open recorder cycle        4 bodies  (live only)
    inside an open recorder cycle         8 bodies  (4 live + 4 ghost)

The fork initializes ghost assignments; ghosts can subsequently act during
an open cycle. They are not frozen bodies. Presence counts above are
upper pools, not counts of available keepers. Availability must account
for holdings, support roles, view restrictions and lifecycle conditions.
NO EXTRACTOR IN SECTION 3 EMITS THIS.  S1-S7 contain nothing that reads
the recorder's cycle structure, so the segment axis of this model rests
on a hand reading of two tech files.  Filed as G8 in
Constraint-Schema-Gaps.txt with the question that should have been
asked.  The numbers above are therefore HAND-DERIVED and carry the
staleness risk M2 exists to prevent.

5  THE BUDGET, AND THE FIRST CONSTRAINT                [COMPLETE,
                                                        REGION-FREE]
------------------------------------------------------------------
AM1  THE BUDGET IS EXACTLY TIGHT.  The five body-cost gates consume
     1 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 3 = 8 plate keepers over disjoint plate sets, and
     the occupant pool is 8.  Opening all five simultaneously requires
     every body in the problem to be standing on a plate.
     Grade 2 (S2's injectivity) joined with grade 1 (S1's table).

AM2  THE AGENT SPENDS ITSELF.  agent1 is one of the eight.  The goal is
     (has-location agent1 location19).  PHYSICAL-SUPPORTS-AT
     (-support-motion.lisp) admits a plate as a support at a location
     only through (has-position ?plate ?location), and no plate is
     positioned at location19 -- the eight plates sit at locations 2,
     7, 10, 13, 12, 16, 17 and 18.  So in the goal state agent1 rests
     on ground, or on a box or tray, but not on a plate.

AM3  THEREFORE, IN THE GOAL STATE:

       reached inside an open cycle   budget 7  ->  at least ONE of
                                                    {gate1, gate3,
                                                     gate4, gate6,
                                                     gate9} is closed
       reached outside a cycle        budget 3  ->  at least TWO of
                                                    those five are
                                                    closed

     The second reading is the sharper one and is arithmetic on the
     cost vector (1,1,1,2,3): three bodies buy at most {gate1, gate3,
     gate4} or {gate9}, never more than three of the five.

AM4  CONDITIONAL COMPETITION WITH THE BEAM. If a required relay must
     use a connector off every simultaneously required plate, the plate
     budget drops by one. An additional incompatible riser role may
     consume another. Gate8 being open alone does not establish either
     premise. CONDITIONAL, pending S5/S6 and role compatibility analysis.
WHAT AM1-AM4 DO NOT SAY.  Nothing here claims any of the five gates
must be open at the goal.  The constraint is of the form "not all of
them", which refutes assignments; it forces nothing.  That is the
logical asymmetry the method is built on, working exactly as stated:
constraints prove impossibility, and this one proves the impossibility
of the fully-open assignment without searching a single action.

     gate7, gate8 and gate9. Their positive pressure requirements include
     plate1, plate2 and plate6/7/8. These are conditional requirements
     during the respective activations, not a simultaneous five-body

AM6  S1 excludes simultaneous opening of gate5 and gate7. Consequently
     the reported graph route cannot be traversed under one fixed setting
     of switch2. A concrete-plan ordering additionally requires proof
     that all relevant concrete movement is covered by that route model.
     Do not promote the graph implication to an unqualified grade-4 theorem.

AM7  Ladder arcs allow empty-handed return in the relaxed graph. Their
     mode predicates and other action conditions still apply: the return
     is not cost-free, and these arcs do not carry held cargo. This says
     nothing by itself about cargo moved through placement or support effects.

AM8  Gate4 labels no traversal arc. Its pressure requirement exists,
     but necessity for a particular beam and therefore for the goal is
     unresolved until sightline semantics establish it. G9 is TAG ONLY;
     do not open it to obtain this information.

AM9  Role inventory cannot be summed into a simultaneous demand without
     a segment and overlap proof. Beam and switch-access roles remain
     conditional, and named bodies require an eligibility/assignment proof.
