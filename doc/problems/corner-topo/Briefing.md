## Contracts
- beam-crossing (UNCOVERED in profile MC; source tech/beam-crossing.lisp):
  - Controls: the derived CROSSING-ACTIVE of each minted crossing point.
  - Rule: a crossing becomes active when both of its beams are live (a transmitter or lit relay feeding a paired
    connector/receiver) and each beam reaches the crossing uncut. A beam is stopped before the crossing by an
    earlier active crossing on it, or by a closed gate that lies before the crossing (BEAM-CROSSINGS-BEFORE-GATE>).
  - Effect: an active crossing cuts every beam through it (BEAM-CUT). A cut segment neither lights the relay nor
    activates the receiver at its far end.
  - Ordering: a beam cut at an earlier crossing (nearer its source) does not reach later crossings on it, so those
    later crossings stay inactive and their other beams survive.
  - Cascades: iterated to a fixed point. Oscillating cases are arbitrated by distance from the source, nearer
    crossing first.
  - Prerequisites: none beyond placement/pairing. It moves or lifts nothing.
- beam-relay (COVERED; recorded for the domain rules used here):
  - pickup-connector clears the connector's pairings.
  - connect-connector places the held connector at the agent's own location. It pairs 1-3 termini that are
    structurally visible from any location the agent can currently walk to.
  - A lit relay passes its single hue.
